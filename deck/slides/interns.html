The core idea

Stop hiring AI interns. Hire a Chief of Staff.

A crowd of interns

  • Ten apps, chatbots and subscriptions
  • Each one starts cold and knows nothing about you
  • You spend the day managing them

One Chief of Staff

  • One agent that holds your full context
  • Reaches your tools and acts on your behalf
  • Gets better the more it knows you
The episode’s framing: leveraging AI doesn’t mean juggling dozens of tools. Most people end up with a crowd of AI interns and spend their day managing them. One tool that knows you well is better than ten that don’t.
