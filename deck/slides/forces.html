The framework

Four forces drain your day

Fragments

Constant app switching splits your attention

Agent role: glue

Flood

A daily avalanche of messages eats your bandwidth

Agent role: gatekeeper

Frequency

Rare recurring tasks slip with no habit to hold them

Agent role: trigger

Future

Busy work crowds out what you actually care about

Agent role: compass

The episode organizes everything around four forces. The first three save time; the fourth decides what that time is for.
